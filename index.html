Thermal Automation • ICF 2026

Temperature-Based Divert Valve System for Ice Bank Protection

During 85°C hot CIP cycles across 13 aging vats, manual drain oversights caused boiling washdown water to bleed into the chilled Ice Banks, causing thermal shock. Deployed an automated Pt100 RTD sensor and REX-C100 controller safety loop.

THERMAL INTERLOCK LOGIC (>7°C THRESHOLD) FAIL-SAFE DUMP
• Temp < 7°C: NC Valve energizes open to Ice Banks (Cold Loop Active)
• Temp > 7°C: Circuit de-energizes → NO Valve springs open to Floor Drain
• Localized visual beacon sirens actuate across the active line path
Sensors: 3-Wire Pt100 RTD Architecture: Modular per 13 Vats
